# Yocare Live channel data

This repository does not promise a public, redistributable IPTV playlist endpoint.

Yocare Live's channel catalogue and playback availability are separate concerns. Channel metadata may originate from third-party datasets, while production playback sources must be separately reviewed for authorisation and territory permissions.

The imported `iptv-source/` directory contains upstream third-party material and its own licence. See [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) and `iptv-source/LICENSE` before using or redistributing any of that material.

Do not treat a public stream URL, playlist entry, or channel logo as evidence that Yocare owns the content or has permission to redistribute it.
