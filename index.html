IDEAS IN MOTION
03 — THE TRUSTCARE INCLUDES YOUR BOUNDARIES

Your inner world is personal

Trust should be
built in, not asked for.

Personal reflections deserve care. Yocare's privacy and security choices must be clear, specific, and verifiable before anyone is asked to trust them.

⌑

Clarity over fine print.

Understand what information a feature uses, why it needs it, and where it goes.

◇

Choice over assumptions.

Meaningful controls should make it easy to decide what to share, keep, or delete.

✳

Safety before spectacle.

Especially for younger users, the experience should be age-aware, respectful, and designed with appropriate safeguards.

Yocare is still in development. These are design commitments, not claims that a production security architecture or data-storage model is already in place.
